## IP-safe transformation rule

When deriving from a luxury-brand reference image:

**Must change (at least three of):**

- Motif content (the figurative or geometric subject).
- Framing device (frame, branch, garland, open).
- Cultural anchor (the heritage story behind the piece).
- Stone palette mix (colours and proportions).

**Preserve only:**

- Richness level (pavé density, multi-cut arrangement, mixed-metal palette).
- Tier (high-jewellery or commercial).
- Format (necklace, ring, brooch, etc.) if the brief calls for it.

**Confusion check:** Would a jeweller looking at the derived piece next to the reference identify them as the same designer's work? If yes, more transformation is needed.
